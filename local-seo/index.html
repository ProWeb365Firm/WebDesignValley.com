    FAQs About Local SEO

    Answers to the questions Minnesota business owners ask us most.

    1. How long does it realistically take to rank on the first page of Google?

    For most local businesses in Minnesota, early progress appears within 3 to 5 weeks of steady work (with an existing website). Solid, dependable results generally take about 3 to 6 months. Exactly how fast SERP ranking happens depends on how many competitors you are up against, the current setup of your website, and how active other similar local businesses are online. Less competitive fields often see faster results, but starting as soon as possible is key. Every month you wait gives your competitors a bigger lead.

    2. What's the difference between SEO and paid ads, and which one do I need?

    Paid ads (like Google Ads) turn on instant visibility, but traffic stops completely the moment your budget runs out. Ads also won’t help your business show up when people search use AI tools like ChatGPT or Perplexity. On the flip side, SEO builds lasting organic rankings that keep working long after the initial setup. For most local service businesses, the best approach is to use both: paid ads bring in quick leads right away, while SEO grows in the background to eventually become your most affordable, long-term source of new customers.

    3. Do I need to show up on AI platforms like ChatGPT and Perplexity, not only Google?

    Yes, you do! The good news is that existing SEO best practices get you halfway there. AI search platforms pull their information from the same places traditional search engines do; your website’s content, Google Business Profile, online reviews, and mentions across the web. If your business ranks well on Google, AI tools are much more likely to recommend you. That’s why having a clean, solid online presence is important.

    4. How important are Google reviews, and do they affect my search ranking?

    Reviews play a large role in who local customers choose to work with. They also send a clear signal to Google that your business is legitimate, active, and trusted. Getting a steady stream of positive, recent reviews can boost your ranking in local Google Map results faster than almost any other strategy. Plus, taking a moment to reply to your reviews (even the negative ones) shows Google and future clients that you care about customer service.

    5. What happens to my rankings if I stop doing SEO?

    Your rankings won’t vanish overnight, but they will slowly slip over time. Search engines favor businesses that consistently update content and earn new reviews. When you pause your efforts, active competitors will gradually pass you by. Think of SEO like maintaining your business reputation.

    6. Can I do my own SEO, or do I really need to hire someone?

    You can easily handle simple tasks yourself, like setting up your Google Business Profile, making sure your phone number and address are consistent across directories, and asking happy customers for reviews. However, the more technical side, like finding the right search keywords, tuning up your web pages, creating helpful content, and tracking data, requires extra time and specialized know-how. Partnering with a professional team takes that heavy lifting off your plate so you can stay focused on running your business.
